Privacy · Confidentialité
  • What is stored: your email address, the finished steps of each session, and your Python code for the exercises.
  • Where: at Supabase, in a data centre in the European Union. Each row can only be read by you (Row Level Security).
  • Why: only to find your progress on another device. No advertising, no tracking, no resale.
  • Videos: nothing is loaded from YouTube until you click “Watch”. The video then plays through YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com).
  • How long: as long as your account exists. The “Delete my account” button erases everything, immediately.
